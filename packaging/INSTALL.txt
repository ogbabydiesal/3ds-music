nodeTrix - 3D Euclidean sequencer + modular synth for Nintendo 3DS homebrew
==========================================================================

REQUIREMENTS
- A Nintendo 3DS / 2DS family console with custom firmware and the
  Homebrew Launcher.
- For sound: the DSP firmware file sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc. Create it once by
  running the "DSP1" homebrew on your console. Without it, nodeTrix runs
  silently (OSC output still works).

INSTALL
1. Copy nodeTrix.3dsx to the /3ds/ folder on your SD card.
2. Put the card back, open the Homebrew Launcher and start nodeTrix.

SOUND FILES
- Put .wav files in sdmc:/3ds/nodeTrix/samples/ (created on first launch) and
  restart nodeTrix. Use them with the SAMPLE node.

YOUR DATA
- Saves (8 slots and the song) are in sdmc:/3ds/nodeTrix/slots/
- Settings are in sdmc:/3ds/nodeTrix/settings.ini
- Network settings for OSC are shared with osc3ds in sdmc:/3ds/osc3ds/config.ini

PRIVACY
nodeTrix collects nothing and contacts no servers. It only sends OSC messages
on your local network, to the IP and port you set, while OSC output is on.
The microphone is used only while a MIC node exists; its audio is played on
the console and never sent or saved.
